llama.cpp / ggml
https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp

The subtitle translation engine the player can start is llama-server from
llama.cpp, shipped in translation/runtime. It is MIT licensed, like ggml
itself, and this notice travels with the copy in the package.

Some of the binaries in that directory additionally link the LLVM OpenMP
runtime; its licence is shipped beside them as LICENSE-LLVM-OpenMP.
