whisper.cpp / ggml
https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp

The speech recognition engine the player can start is whisper-server from
whisper.cpp, shipped twice: once as asr/runtime (the upstream build, which
recognises on the processor) and once as asr/runtime-gpu (the same build with
ggml-vulkan.dll beside it, which recognises on the graphics card). Whichever
the Subtitles menu selects is the one that starts.

whisper.cpp is MIT licensed, like ggml itself, and this notice travels with the
copy in the package. Two of the DLLs staged beside the server are not part of
whisper.cpp and carry their own permits:

  libopenblas.dll - OpenBLAS, BSD 3-Clause
                    https://github.com/OpenMathLib/OpenBLAS
  SDL2.dll        - Simple DirectMedia Layer, zlib
                    https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL

No model is shipped. The recogniser needs one in asr/models, which the user
supplies; see the notice beside it.
